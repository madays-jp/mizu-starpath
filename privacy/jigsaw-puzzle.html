Privacy Policy

MA DAYS ("we") provides the app "Mizu Jigsaw Puzzle" (Japanese title: ミズーのえあわせパズル, the "App"). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any data, including personal information such as your name, email address, location, or contacts. No data is sent to our servers or to any third party, and no account registration is required.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores your game records — puzzle progress, completed pictures, and display settings for the viewing ROOM — only in the App's storage on your device. These records never leave your device. The App has no cloud sync, export, or import features, and its records are deleted when you delete the App.

3. Tracking, Advertising, and Analytics

The App does not track you. It shows no advertising, contains no advertising SDKs and no in-app purchases, and does not use analytics services.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the App collects no data, there is no data to share with third parties. Distribution and updates of the App through the App Store or TestFlight, and information handling by iOS and Apple's services, are governed by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact:

Email: project30.work@gmail.com

If you send us an email, your email address and message reach our support desk. We use them only to respond to your inquiry and to investigate and improve issues, keep them only as long as needed, and delete them on request.

6. Changes

If a future update changes how information is handled (for example, if advertising or purchase features are added), we will revise this policy and the store declarations accordingly and publish the updated version on this page.
